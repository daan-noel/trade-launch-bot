# Hit rate and cover: open work

Whether a spelling is a trader's rule is its hit rate and cover
([derive 5.0](../plans/strategies/_!___derive.md), proof in
[evidence 5.8](../plans/strategies/_!___evidence.md)). The counter is
[toolkit/hitrate.py](../plans/strategies/node-derivation/toolkit/README.md). What does not read
it yet:

1. **Unscored ideas.** Every inventory status and case-file verdict marked "unscored: needs a
   hit-rate read (derive 5.0)" is read with the counter, 8dtx2t first (its E ladder is item 1
   of its unread list).
2. **Entry Context.** The market scan ([entry-context-scan.md](../plans/strategies/entry-context-scan.md))
   lists every moment the idea holds and his next buy after it. Counted as chances (the rising
   edge while he is free) and hits (his next buy within W), it shows the hit rate and cover of
   the idea on the page.
