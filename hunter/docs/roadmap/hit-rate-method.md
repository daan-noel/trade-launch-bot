# Hit rate and cover: open work

Whether a spelling is a trader's rule is its hit rate and cover
([derive 5.0](../plans/strategies/_!___derive.md), proof in
[evidence 5.8](../plans/strategies/_!___evidence.md)). The counter is
[toolkit/hitrate.py](../plans/strategies/node-derivation/toolkit/README.md). What does not read
it yet:

1. **The E ladder.** `walkforward.ladder` and `contrast` score a step on the acted label of a
   his-coins candidate table. Derive 6.1 scores it on the hit rate of every coin's chances:
   the ladder needs `hitrate.measure` as its step score, the chance ladder shuffling his
   entries across whole coins, and the 30-hit floor.
2. **One set of facts.** `hitrate` runs on the curve tape of `mid-tape/hr_prep.py`, and
   `mid-tape/hr_core.py` recomputes the quiet gap, the print's move and the new-wallet flag
   that `toolkit.facts` already computes on a `tapes.Session`. The counter has to read a
   `tapes.Session` and those facts, with a parity check against `hr_core` on 8dtx2t's E.
3. **Engine parity.** A spelling measured here is not yet the engine's rule: its facts are
   Python. The hit rate of a rule the engine can run is read on the engine's own
   evaluation, not on a Python copy.
4. **Unscored ideas.** Every inventory status and case-file verdict marked "unscored: needs a
   hit-rate read (derive 5.0)" is read with the counter, 8dtx2t first (its E ladder is item 1
   of its unread list).
5. **Flow Discovery.** `flow_discovery.rs` ranks a group's structures by `group_lift`
   (`share(S|group) / share(S|window)`), a per-moment lift of who the group answers. That
   ranking needs hit rate and cover. Metric Discovery's and `family_search`'s `lift` is a money
   delta against a baseline book, a money read, and stays.
6. **Entry Context.** The market scan ([entry-context-scan.md](../plans/strategies/entry-context-scan.md))
   lists every moment the idea holds and his next buy after it. Counted as chances (the rising
   edge while he is free) and hits (his next buy within W), it shows the hit rate and cover of
   the idea on the page.
