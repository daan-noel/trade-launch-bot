# 7ix owner paths

Fit week: coins of the new create list, CU price 1000, born in the latest 7 days
(250 coins). The prior week in the 14-day window is the old create list. Snipers,
buy-only wallets, group wallets, and instruction lists are counted on both.

The split follows [_!___owner-split.md](../_!___owner-split.md). It marks 91% of the SOL as
owner. The owner finishes ahead of outsiders on 97% of the coins. Paths below are
read from that split, on the fit week only, by the method in
[owner-decisions.md](../owner-decisions.md).

## The four paths

Every fit-week coin is one of these. The readings are the last print before the
owner's fall. Pool is virtual SOL. Migration is 115.

| path | coins | age | outsider SOL | pool | what the owner is doing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| soon | 76 | 9s | 2 | 48 | real money never arrives, the owner dumps the first push |
| short run | 132 | 41s | 5 | 60 | a small crowd pays, the owner dumps that profit |
| near the curve | 17 | 7 min | 37 | 108 | a real crowd pays, the owner sells while the pool can still take the bag |
| migrate | 22 | | | 115 | the owner pushes the coin through the curve |
| no life | 3 | | | 31 | the pool never leaves the open |

The soon path and the short run are on every day of the fit week that has more
than a handful of coins. The near-curve path is on 6 of the 8 days. Migration is
on every day.

Outsider SOL at the dump holds across the week: about 0.3 to 2.7 SOL on the soon
path, about 3 to 8 SOL on the short run. Owner profit does not. Through Oct 1 the
soon dump takes about 3 SOL of profit and the short run about 10. From Oct 2 the
soon dump takes about 11 and the short run about 25 to 50. The path is the same.
The profit level is the part that moves.

## The branch

Every second he still holds, the next keep-or-dump act is another buy. A dump is the next act on about 1% of those seconds. Flow dying, a flat price, and a thin return on his last push do not raise that. On every pool band, a dying flow dumps less often than a flow that is still arriving, on every day of the fit week.

The two seconds before a dump do differ from the two seconds before another buy. Outsiders have bought about 1 SOL in the last 3 seconds before a dump, and about 0.1 SOL before another buy. Under a pool of 50, a last-3-second total of 1 SOL or more is followed by the dump 22% of the time, and a smaller total 1% of the time, on 6 of 7 days. He still buys again on the other 78%. From a pool of 70 up, that gap fades.

So a burst of outsider buys sits under the dumps that happen. It does not make the dump the usual next act. No reading in this pass becomes a stage.

## Which coin takes which path

The fit week uses 238 creator wallets. A wallet does not launch a second coin inside 10 minutes, so the session is not on the creator wallet.

The gap since the previous 7ix coin, any creator, leaves the soon share near the base rate. Under 2 minutes it is 30% (77 coins). From 2 to 10 minutes it is 23% (108 coins). A gap over 30 minutes is 44% soon (39 coins), and a coin within 10 minutes is the soon side on only 2 of 7 days.

No 7ix coin is followed by another within 3 seconds. The next launch is not on the tape before a soon dump.

The first outsider buy by 3 seconds moves the share. Under 0.5 SOL the soon share is 23% (199 coins). From 0.5 to 1 SOL it is 47% (34 coins), higher on 4 of 5 days that have both. The soon path is still not the usual one.

No reading known by 3 seconds puts the soon dumps on one side and the coins he keeps working on the other.

## The end of a push

95% of the gaps between his buys are 3 seconds or less. A push is a run of his buys with no gap over 1 second. The row is the last buy. The label is the next act.

He buys again. The first push does it 95% of the time (248 pushes). Outsider SOL, profit, and pool match on the dumps and on the buys: about 0.1 SOL, about 9 SOL of profit, pool 47. A later push does it 99% of the time.

A later push under a pool of 50 that already holds 1 SOL of outsider buying is followed by the dump 66% of the time (41 pushes). 30 of those 41 are on Oct 2. The other days are a handful of pushes. A cut that sits on one day is not a logic. At 0.5 SOL the dump share is 48% (61 pushes), the higher side on 2 of 4 days.

A gap of 3 seconds glues a dump's whole rise into one row. That row lasts about 15 seconds and holds about 2.5 SOL of outsider buying. The first push he continues lasts about 0.3 seconds and holds about 0.4 SOL. Profit and pool still match, about 11 against about 9, pool 50 against 47. The longer row is the ending.

No end-of-push reading makes the fall the usual next act on most days of the fit week.

## His own open

The first push is his buys until a gap over 1 second. Soon, the short run, and migration use the same open: about 16.5 SOL, 5 buys, a bag of about 26 SOL. From Oct 2 that size is the same number on all three.

An open of 25 SOL or more is the near-curve path on 9 of 19 coins, against 4% at the script size of 14 to 20 SOL. One day has enough coins on both sides. An open under 10 SOL is soon on 46% of 61 coins, against 27% at the script size, on 2 of 2 days with both. The short run stays the usual path on the script size, at 60%.

Entering every coin at the end of that push, and selling 0.115 seconds after the dump, books +2.4 SOL on 247 coins of 0.03 SOL. Entering only the heavy opens books +0.4 SOL. Entering only the script size books +1.7 SOL. Entering only the light opens books +0.3 SOL. No open filter books more than entering every coin.

## The decision

He keeps buying until the dump prints. No earlier reading makes one next act the usual one on most days of the fit week. The exit is that print. There is no entry filter.

## What the tape does not support

Outsider buying by 5 seconds does not change the chance of a soon dump. Coins
under 0.5 SOL and coins over 0.5 SOL both dump soon about a quarter of the time.

At a pool line (45, 55, 70, 90, 105), coins that dump within 20 seconds and coins
that keep going show the same outsider SOL, the same profit, and the same age.
A dying flow is when he buys again.

At pool 100 the near-curve coins and the migration coins carry about the same
profit (about 160 SOL). Outsider SOL is about 12 against about 19. That gap is
too small, on 15 and 22 coins, to be a gate.

A print whose next act is a partial sell after a large 30 second outsider total
loses to selling on the owner's fall. It is not a stage.

## The exit the lead allows

The one-shot fall is labeled on the prints inside the last second (median lead
0.6 seconds, the short end 0.2 seconds). A fill of 0.115 seconds lands on that
print. When the lead is shorter, the sell is the dump print itself.

No earlier reading in this run books more than that print.
