# Spend 3 owner paths

The group is the create list `Pump.Fun: Create_v2`, `Associated Token: Create`,
`Pump.Fun: BuyExactSolIn`, with spendable SOL 3. The fit week is the latest 7
days of prints: coins born 2026-09-30 through 2026-10-05. 795 coins. 2026-10-05
has 23.

The owner is the stored `volume` tag on `spend=3 · 3ix:BuyExactSolIn`. He is
95% of the SOL. The median coin is 97% owner, and he finishes ahead of
outsiders on 719 of the 795 coins. The shape rows carry 52,614 SOL, the creator
6,856, the wallet list 1,629.

Paths below are read from that tag, on the fit week only, by the method in
[owner-decisions.md](../owner-decisions.md).

## What he is doing

He launches the same way on every coin. The create buy is 3.0 SOL, and the
first push adds more of his own buys in the same moment. The pool opens at 33
and sits near 40 before a real buyer can act.

After that he paints. A push is a run of his buys with no gap over 1 second.
The first one lasts 0.3 seconds and spends 8.2 SOL. A later one spends 1.5.
Between the lifts he sells a sliver, about 5% of the bag on a short coin and
about 1% on a long one. The median coin has 26 of his buys and 14 of those
slivers.

He always leaves in one burst. A dump is an owner sell that takes at least half
the bag within 2 seconds. 790 coins end that way. 5 reach the curve. None fade
out.

The net is the median coin, in SOL, sells plus the bag he still holds minus
what he spent. The five migrations sum to 1,158 SOL. The other 790 coins sum
to about 700, a few tenths each. Outsiders have put in about 0.6 SOL when the
typical coin dumps. The paper profit before that dump is his own push.

The short dump is on every day: 53, 69, 62, 64, 53, and 39 percent of that
day's dumps. The median age moves between 4 and 12 seconds. The net stays
about flat.

## The decision

At 3 seconds, 559 coins are still his. Outsider SOL under 0.3 leaves the coin
alive past 30 seconds on 36% of 331 coins, and that is the higher side on all
6 days. Outsider SOL from 0.3 to 1 leaves it alive past 30 seconds on 17% of
213 coins. An early bid is a reason to cash out. Silence is a reason to lift
again. Half of the quiet coins are still dumped before 15 seconds, so silence
does not make the long life the usual one.

While he still holds, 15,275 seconds, the next trade is another buy on 49% and
a sliver sell on 33%. The dump is the next trade on 3%. A pool under 40 raises
that to 16%, and he still buys again on 64%, on every day the cell has. A pool
above 55 leaves the dump on 0-1% of seconds. His profit being large, a burst
of outsider buys, a flat price, and a second coin of his in the last 3 minutes
do not make the dump the usual next trade.

A buy of 0.03 SOL at 1 second, sold 0.115 seconds after his cash-out, books
+1.52 SOL on 685 coins. The median coin loses. The dumps under 10 seconds book
-1.20. The longer dumps book +2.72. No earlier reading makes the dump the
usual next act, so no earlier sell is a stage.

He keeps buying, and skimming, until the cash-out prints. There is no entry
filter. The exit is that print.
