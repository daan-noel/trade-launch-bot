# 6ix max=0.65 owner paths

The group is `6ix:Buy · cu_price=1K · max=0.65`. 255 coins, born 2026-09-30
through 2026-10-11. The split follows
[_!___owner-split.md](../_!___owner-split.md). Paths follow
[owner-decisions.md](../owner-decisions.md).

The fit week is the 74 coins born 2026-10-05, 2026-10-10, and 2026-10-11.

## The life

The open is a script: buy 0.49, then buy 15.80, pool 30 to 46. About a second
later he skims 1.15. Then the rise, in two stages on every coin that goes high.

| stage | pool | speed | time to cross |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| fast rise | 46 to 90 | 0.2 to 0.3 SOL/s | about 3 min |
| slow rise | 90 to 115 | 0.05 SOL/s | about 9 min |

74 coins reach 70. The 20 that migrate all show both stages. Coins that dump
before 90 show only the fast rise.

## Two exit conditions

**Outsider burst, on the fast rise.** A dump while the pool is still climbing
fast (67 coins, age p50 12 s) has 1.1 SOL and 4 outsider wallets in the prior
3 seconds. The last of those buys is 1.1 s before the dump. None of these
dumps is quiet for 10 s. The earliest dumps are a crowd of small buys: on the
fit week, under pool 50, 3 or more wallets on 72% and a 1 SOL total on none.
The burst is that crowd. He also buys through other bursts, so this condition
is his decision on those dumps, and it is not every burst.

**The dump instruction, on every coin.** Every dump is the `9ddjzq` dump
instruction, about 20 transactions in one slot, about 22 SOL, 99% of the bag.
This is the exit when the burst condition did not fire, including the slow rise.

## The rule

Enter 0.03 SOL when the pool reaches 55 and the last 3 seconds have at most 16
prints. Scale out of the initial bag: 30% at pool 62, 20% at 80, 15% at 100.
The remaining 35% sells on the dump instruction, or 270 s after the first AMM
print.

128 coins. Curve result +0.52 SOL, median -6%, mean +14%. 26 coins lose 50%
or more. 16 coins are still held at the line, median +166% on those, and they
are +0.80 SOL of the total. Holding the whole bag from the same entry, with
no scale-out, is +0.91 SOL, median -33%, and 52 coins lose 50% or more. Same
16 coins at the line.

19 coins touch the line. 3 print the dump instruction first, so the stub is
already sold and 16 are the migration count of the curve book. On the AMM tape
the same stub, sold once at 270 s, returns 1.23× the migration print (median
1.20×, worst coin 0.82×).

## Booked

Entry is the first print at pool 48. Size 0.03 SOL, fill 115 ms later, curve
fee 125 bps each leg. Two exits: a burst of 3 or more outsider wallets in 3
seconds while the pool is under 90 and the prior 10 seconds are still climbing
at 0.15 SOL/s or more, otherwise the dump instruction.

| exit | coins | result |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| dump instruction | 225 | +0.74 SOL |
| burst, else the dump instruction | 225 | +0.30 SOL |
| fit week, dump instruction | 77 | -0.05 SOL |
| fit week, burst else dump | 77 | +0.18 SOL |

The burst fires on 215 of 225 coins. The lead to the dump is 20 s (23 s on the
fit week). The first burst of the coin is never the dump. A tighter burst
(more wallets, more SOL) still leaves the dump as the next act on under 13% of
those moments. The dump instruction is the exit that books on the whole set.
The fit week is the other way, by 0.23 SOL, with the same early lead.

## Flow by where the coin ends

Curve prints only. A migrator's bag is still unsold here, so his curve cash is
the clips minus the SOL still in the bag.

| case | coins | peak | owner buy | partial sells | dump sell | curve cash | outsider buy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| dump in the open | 34 | 47 | 17 | 1.5 | 16 | +0.5 | 2.0 |
| dump in the fast rise | 188 | 55 | 35 | 15 | 24 | +1.7 | 4.5 |
| dump in the slow rise | 12 | 107 | 288 | 218 | 74 | +5.7 | 16 |
| migrate | 20 | 115 | 504 | 421 | 0 | -75 | 30 |

Of coins that enter the fast rise, 188 of 224 dump there. Of coins that reach
90, 20 migrate and 12 dump. Partials are 47% of his buys on a fast dump, 76%
on a slow dump, and 85% on a migrator.

## The slow rise

33 coins reach pool 90 before a dump. 17 then dump, 16 migrate. The dumps sit
at pool 106, 3 minutes after the cross. Outsider wallets in the prior 3
seconds are 0. The burst is absent. The migrators take 8 minutes and cross
115. No other reading separates the two. The exit on this stage is the dump
instruction.
