**The rule in one sentence.** Buy a listed bot's push on a coin under 38 s old when its launch template
has lately made steady risers more often than today's market, the push is big for this coin, and brand
new wallets already hold part of the float; sell when buying stops for 10 s, else ride a trail that
widens with age, at most 600 s.

### How it was chosen

The choice rule was written before the read: money > 0 in each of block A (09-01 .. 09-07), B
(09-08 .. 09-14) and C (09-15 .. 09-21); >= 50 trades on the thinnest day; then the most positive days;
then the most money. Pool: slot SOL >= 0.391 (it keeps 90 % of his fit-day picks). Nine signals at two
levels each, every conjunction of 1 - 4 (2,850 rules, 758 with enough trades, 47 pass): the pusher's and
the structure's 7-day money record, the top ten's share of the float (<= 0.95 / 0.90), the Door (1.164 /
1.676), age (<= 38 / 120 s), busy tape (12+ structures / 13+ buys in 60 s), push size (1.5 / 3x), reserve
(<= 44.2 / 38), fresh (0.034 / 0.116). The first row is the pool; the exit was re-tuned on it by the same
rule over 72 spellings (pause always / only under 0 or -2.7 % / up to 20, 30, 60 s then under -2.7 %; trail
x0.8 / 1 / 1.3, ceiling 20 / 30 / 40 % / none), nearly all of which pass.

Close alternates (all pass; pool terms, exit x1.3 / 40 %): Door 1.164 + push 1.5x + reserve 38 + fresh

### The confirmation read (written before it)

R3 as frozen (`data/w68_frozen.json`) is read ONCE on untouched days from 09-22 on. It passes when all
hold: at least 5 days; money a trade > 0 over them; positive on at least 60 % of them; no single day
more than half of the profit.

### Known limits

- One trade per coin, in the launch phase: no re-entry, and about 105 trades a day.
- Most of it is one mainstream template; a change in pump.fun's creation flow moves its main group.
- The launch minute is the most crowded: a buy can fail its slippage guard or land deeper in the slot
  than the tape assumes. Only a small live test reads that.
- Fresh wallets on a tape's first days include wallets active before the tape; both tapes alike.

### How to update R3

Scripts (local, `mid-tape/r3/`, git-ignored like every study script): `r3_tape_export.py` (a tape from the
lake, day by day), `r3_facts.py <tape>` (the push table and its facts, `w1_<tape>/`), `r3_prep.py` (money
and exit fill per push, pusher address, structure hash, the Door, `data/w65_<tape>.parquet`),
`r3_holders.py <tape>` (top ten, fresh wallets), `r3_search.py` (every conjunction under the written rule,
`data/w67_dev21.csv`), `r3_exit.py` (the exit re-tuned on a pool, `data/w68_frozen.json`), `r3_speed.py`
(the speed test), `r3_groups.py` (the launch-group split), `r3_load.py` (the reader).

1. New days: export and register a tape (`toolkit/tapes.py`), then `r3_facts.py`, `r3_prep.py` (its tape
   list), `r3_holders.py`.
2. A new signal: add its column to `X` and two levels to `SIG` in `r3_search.py`; the rule and the blocks
   stay as written; rerun and read the first row.
3. The exit on a new pool: set `RULE` in `r3_exit.py`; then `r3_speed.py` must stay above zero at 250 ms.
4. Freeze, then read once on days no step has touched, with the gate written first.
