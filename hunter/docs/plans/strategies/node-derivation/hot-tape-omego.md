## 1. His logic (the portrait, derive 3)

> He buys a **cheap coin that has just come off its own recent high by a little** - 5-15 % under
> it, not at it and not deep below - with a **real but not enormous crowd already on it**, answering
> a public print within **50-70 ms**, because he expects the next wave of buyers to arrive inside
> seconds; he skips the coin nobody has found yet and the coin that already topped out far above
> him; he leaves when a sell lands into a tape that has stopped buying, because then the wave is
> over (section 2).

**Which half carries the weight: the coin.** Holding everything else, the crowd on the coin moves
his result from **-2.82 %/trade** (under 30 buyers) to **+5.05 %** (60-120), and where the coin sits
against its own high moves it from **-0.83 %** (at the high) to **+3.29 %** (5-15 % under). That
routes step 6 to open **D first** (derive 7.0).

| clause | slot | his likely reason | the yes / no test (written before the run) | step | answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the coin is a little under its own recent high, not at it and not far below | D / P | at the high there is nobody left to pay up; far below, the buyers who made the high are gone | does his %/trade rise and fall with that distance across every entry, and does the same hold on coins he skips? | 6.1, 7 | the first half is measured (C2); the second is unread |
| a crowd exists on the coin but is not yet a mob | D / P | nobody on it means nobody to lift it; a mob means the lift is spent | the same, over the count of public buyers before his fill | 6.1, 7 | first half measured (C2) |
| he answers a public print within 50-70 ms, two thirds of them buys | E | somebody just committed money and more is following | does buying follow those prints more than other prints on the same coin? | 5.1 | not run |
| a sell into a tape that has stopped buying is the end | X | the wave he bought is over | does that branch name his sells, and does the set book his own close? | 8.0 | **red** (B1-B9) |

**Not two wallets, one.** The fast and the slow halves of his book enter on the **same** facts -
prior-peak ratio 1.12 against 1.16, crowd 313 against 275 - and differ only in what happens after:
11 public buys land during a fast hold against 23 during a slow one, and 0.39 % against 2.43 % of
them reach +100 %. The hold is the consequence, not a second decision, so this file does **not**
split (law 27 asked and answered).

**The seat problem is already visible.** His lag behind the print he answers is p50 **52-70 ms**,
and our own fill lands at 83-115 ms. Step 5 is where that is settled and it is the question that
decides this wallet.

**Who he is, from the tape:** a bot. One leg per transaction on every one of 55,185 legs, a direct
pump.fun call through four ix structures (`Buy` / `BuyV2` / `Sell` / `SellV2`, 99.8 % of legs), a
fixed fee preset every day (cu_price 500,000, tip 200,000 lamports), and `SetComputeUnitPrice`
before `SetComputeUnitLimit`. No router, no aggregator, no bundling.

as two groups. They turn out to be one logic with two outcomes (section 1, C2).

### The 20 trades (derive 3.2, `om12_portrait.py`)

The 10 best by SOL and the 10 losers nearest his median loss, picked by that rule and never by eye;
then five and five inside each half of his hold. The full tables are `data/om12_trades.parquet`.

**What they show, and it is a hypothesis:** his ten best all enter a coin that has **never been
higher** (entry vsol 38-50 against a prior peak of the same 38-50), with a small crowd (16-228
buyers), and 52-337 public buys land while he holds; not one goes meaningfully under his fill. His
typical losers enter a coin that already topped out far above where he buys (entry vsol 38-70
against a prior peak of 60-110), with a crowd already in it (261-1,162), and 0-21 buys land while
he holds.

**Checked over all 27,372 entries, half of that is wrong** (C2). Buying at the coin's own high is
his **worst** band, not his best. The 20 are a lottery sample: 386 trades reach +100 % and carry
69 % of his net, so picking the ten largest picks the tail and not the logic - which is exactly why
derive 3.2 pairs them with ten ordinary losers and why a portrait is a hypothesis until it is
checked.

## 2. His exit (derive 8.0)

**Named in words, red as a rule.** At the RACE seat his exit is worth **+120.09 SOL** over a 15 s
clock on the same 27,372 entries (A4), which is why it was read before anything else.

> He leaves on a public sell that lands into a tape which has **stopped buying** - a run of sells,
> few ix structures still printing, little public buy SOL in the last seconds, the price rolling
> over - and he sits through a sell that lands into a live tape.

That sentence is measured (B5, both folds) and **it does not reproduce his close in any form the
method's vocabulary can write**. Three were tried and all three are red (B6, B8), the explanation
first offered for the failure is itself refuted (B7), and the one decision that would have excused
it - a book-level exit no per-coin state could ever name - is ruled out (B9).

| what is established | chain row |
| --- | --- |
| it is not a fixed rule - every spread is wide (close p10..p90 -18.8..+26.6 %, give-back 0..25.6 %, hold 1.7..133 s) | B1 |
| the trigger is a public print stepping price **down**, answered in **25-50 ms** (lift 5.37 against the prints he sits through) | B2 |
| there is no fixed target and no fixed stop; the hazard falls with time in the profit bands and rises with time in the loss bands | B2 |
| no single branch names more than **9.7 %** of his sells; he sits through about **70 %** of the crossings | B3 |
| of the down-prints inside one hold, the one he takes lands into a **stopped tape**, both folds agreeing | B5 |
| that state reads **absolute**, not in the coin's own units - the ratio spellings are weaker | B7 |
| it gates his **fast** holds twice as hard as his slow ones (-27.6 against -9.9 on structures in 5 s) | B7 |
| no branch, no conjunction of that state, and no fitted score over it books near his close | B6, B8 |
| he does **not** close in batches, so the exit is a per-coin decision and the failure is not the grain | B9 |

**The verdict: his exit is not readable at the grain this program can write.** The nearest
description is a plain 5 % trail at **0.0250 SOL a trade** from his close (corr 0.466), against
0.0061 for 9Uq8GV's clock and 0.0098 for 8dtx2t's curved trail - four times further at half the
correlation. The holdout is **not** spent on it: nothing passed the fit-day bar, so there is
nothing to confirm (8.0 e).

**What that does and does not block.** Derive 6.2 covers this case: where 8.0 finds no rule, an
entry is priced under a hold-matched clock **written as a placeholder that decides nothing**, so a
red entry book under it closes nothing. And the exit slot on his pool is not the program's problem
here, because several simple public exits **beat** him on his own entries (B6a).

## 3. Rule (unnumbered)

**The sentence, written out slot by slot, and refuted with a ceiling.** Empty is a value, and so
is red: this is what the six slots hold after the structure event is spelled to the end.

> **D** anything. **E** a public buy of 0.25 SOL or more whose transaction is one of 15 named
> `build_core` structures, as the print immediately before us. **P** that print's own price step
> under 2.045 %. **X** out at 5 s unless the trade is 5 % up, then ride while public buying
> continues, stop -25 %, cap 300 s. **R** fire only while flat on the coin and past our first exit
> there. **S** one clip.

It books **-3.06 %/trade, 0 of 6 days, on 66,763 coins** at our seat, which is the best of fifteen
exits, and **-1.62 % even when the prints the member answers are picked by hindsight**. That is
not a proof that no slot work can carry it - E5 withdraws that step, because on this pool the
top 1 % of fires is positive and the body bleeds, and an average does not bound a subset. The same prints pay **+2.07 %/trade 6 of 6 days** at a fill that
meets the reserve the trigger print met, and **-1.27 % at ten milliseconds** after it: the edge is
that print's own impact and there is no seat that holds it (E2e).

different and more final gate.
